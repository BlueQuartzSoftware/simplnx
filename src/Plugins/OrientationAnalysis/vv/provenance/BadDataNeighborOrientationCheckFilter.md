## How it was generated

The 27 synthetic fixtures use hand-selected orientations, phases and initial masks: 18 base cases, eight propagation cases and one mixed-phase case. Per-case CSV files and input pipelines define the data; the design and generation method are in `vv_work:BadDataNeighborOrientationCheckFilter/notes/engineer_test_design.md` and `vv_work:BadDataNeighborOrientationCheckFilter/notes/engineer_archive_README.md`. For the A/B comparison, `vv_work:BadDataNeighborOrientationCheckFilter/scripts/make_fixture_inputs.py` copies the fixture arrays into shared legacy-format inputs. `vv_work:BadDataNeighborOrientationCheckFilter/comparison/archive_audit.json` confirms that all 27 fixture cell arrays match both the CSVs and the original NX inputs.

The test inputs contain Quats, Phases and Mask under `Image Geometry/Cell Data`, and Crystal Structures under `Image Geometry/Cell Ensemble Data`. Case 1 uses 3×3×3 images; Cases 2, 3 and 4 use 5×5×5 images. Expected output masks are defined in C++, not loaded from a computed output file.

## Regenerated to fix a circular-oracle situation?

The canonical masks remain the analytical C++ expectations. PR #1631 corrects tolerance conversion and registers the v2 input archive. Cases 1.3.3, 1.4.3, 1.5.3 and 1.6.3 keep the center bad.

`vv_work:BadDataNeighborOrientationCheckFilter/algorithm_review/source_comparison.md` gives the numerical boundary evidence. At 5°, float tolerance conversion gives `0.0872664675116539` radians; double conversion gives `0.087266462599716474`. The stored quaternions produce a double angle of `0.087266463155491913`, slightly above the tolerance. `vv_work:BadDataNeighborOrientationCheckFilter/comparison/legacy_emulator_results.md` isolates 37 phase-gating differences and four tolerance differences in a local proof version; the final compiled proof removes all 41 differences and matches all 27 fixture masks.
