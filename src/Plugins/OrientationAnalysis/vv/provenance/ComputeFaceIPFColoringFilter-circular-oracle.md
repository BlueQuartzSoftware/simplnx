## How it was generated

The archive supplies `6_6_Small_IN100_GBCD/6_6_Small_IN100_GBCD.dream3d`, a Small IN100 dataset with surface-mesh and feature arrays. The current tests load those arrays for invalid-selection checks and three color-key executions. Their independent analytical inputs are constructed in `src/Plugins/OrientationAnalysis/test/ComputeFaceIPFColoringTest.cpp`, rather than read from the archive.

The separate legacy comparison fixture is synthetic. `vv_work:ComputeFaceIPFColoringFilter/build_fixture.py` creates four faces with cubic and hexagonal features, identity Euler angles and corner-aligned normals. `vv_work:ComputeFaceIPFColoringFilter/legacy_comparison_summary.md` records its 2026-06-30 results; the deviations sidecar preserves those results and identifies the later algorithm changes.

The five-face fixture uses feature orientations `(pi/2,0,0)`, `(pi/3,0,0)` and `(0,0,0)`. Cubic symmetry preserves the red `<100>` and blue `<111>` corners under the first rotation. The hexagonal rotation preserves its basal green and c-axis red corners. Exact basal green follows the current TSL convention; the zero-red invariant is independent of the green/blue assignment. All pass in CI at `419f9db35`.

## Regenerated to fix a circular-oracle situation?

The archive was not regenerated. PR #1646 replaces `Valid filter execution`, which compared both sides with the archived `SurfaceMeshFaceIPFColors`, with `Class 1 Oracle - mixed-phase analytical`. The archived array carries the same wrong-phase defect as the old algorithm, so agreement with it could not detect D1. The 2026-06-30 comparison summary records the 120,000 exterior-face differences after correction.

The shared archive remains an input to the invalid-selection and color-key tests. Its `SurfaceMeshFaceIPFColors` array is no longer this filter's oracle. The change and the replacement fixture are visible in commit `f5b383be6` (PR #1646).
