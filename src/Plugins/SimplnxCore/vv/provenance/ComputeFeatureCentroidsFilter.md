## How it was generated

`CentroidToy::Build()` creates an image, explicit FeatureIds and a feature AttributeMatrix from each fixture's dimensions, spacing and origin. Expected values are hand-derived constants in `RequireCentroid()` calls. The bulk fixture constructs 65,537 cells with non-unit spacing and nonzero origin; its failure variant supplies a store that rejects its second bulk read.
